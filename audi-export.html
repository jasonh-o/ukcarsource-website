How It Works

Export Your Audi

Send us the model, quantity and your port. We confirm the car and the price in a written quote, CFR to your port, or CIF with marine insurance on request.

Questions

Audi Export FAQ

Are the new Audi prices fixed?

Export prices exclude UK VAT, inland logistics and shipping. Duty and local taxes are paid by the buyer on arrival. Prices are a guide. Final price and specification depend on the stock available when you enquire, and are confirmed in your quote.

Are these new Audi cars registered?

Yes. They are in brand-new condition, with delivery mileage only. Each car is registered in the UK before export as part of the supply terms. Some countries treat a registered car as used, so check with your customs authority or clearing agent.

Do the cars come with an Audi warranty?

UK-specification cars are supplied for export without manufacturer warranty. Servicing and parts are arranged locally by the buyer. We can also supply parts from the UK.

Can you source a used Audi?

Yes. We source new and used right-hand drive Audi models in the UK to order, including the A3, A4, A5, A6, Q3, Q5, Q7, Q8, e-tron GT and S and RS models. Tell us the model, year, mileage and budget.

Can you supply a left-hand drive Audi?

Left-hand drive Rolls-Royce is supplied to order from the UK. All other makes, new or used, are sourced in Germany and across Europe.

How do I pay?

Pay by Letter of Credit, or by deposit with the balance before shipping. We quote CFR to your port, or CIF with marine insurance on request.
