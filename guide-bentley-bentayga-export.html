Export Guide

Exporting a Bentley Bentayga from the UK

Built in Crewe, England, the Bentley Bentayga is the world's most refined luxury SUV — and buying one through a UK export specialist is significantly cheaper than purchasing from an authorised dealer overseas. This guide covers the full process from pricing to port delivery.

Talk to Our Team

Why Export a Bentayga from the UK?

The Bentley Bentayga is handbuilt at the Crewe factory in Cheshire — just like every Bentley Continental GT and Flying Spur. Bentley Motor Cars has manufactured in Crewe since 1946, and this heritage is baked into the brand's identity globally. The vehicle that a buyer in Kuwait or Singapore orders from their local Bentley dealer came from exactly the same production line as one ordered by a UK buyer. The difference is entirely in the price paid.

For non-UK buyers, the UK's export pricing structure — combined with Bentley's competitive factory positioning in its home market and favourable exchange rates — means the total cost of acquiring a Bentayga through the UK is often substantially below the equivalent vehicle purchased locally in markets like Singapore, Malaysia, or Nigeria. The exact saving depends on the specific vehicle, its VAT status, and your destination market, but the UK advantage is consistently significant.

Beyond pricing, the UK market offers access to the full Mulliner bespoke programme. Bentley's Mulliner division — essentially the ultra-bespoke arm of the brand — can commission virtually any colour combination, interior material, veneer, and personalisation feature imaginable. UK Bentley dealers have experienced Mulliner specialists and strong factory relationships. Commissioning a Bentayga Mulliner through a UK dealer, for export, is one of the finest luxury car purchasing experiences available.

UK Export Pricing and VAT

For new Bentaygas purchased directly from a Bentley UK authorised dealer for export, the sale is zero-rated for VAT — meaning the buyer does not pay the VAT component embedded in the UK showroom price. This is the cleanest route for new vehicles. The UK list prices in the table above are the starting point; the actual price you pay will depend on specification, the specific vehicle's VAT position, and any negotiation with the supplying dealer.

For used Bentaygas, the VAT position varies by vehicle. Most used prestige vehicles in the UK are sold under the VAT Margin Scheme — where VAT applies only to the dealer's margin, not the full vehicle value. This is standard practice in the UK used car trade and is reflected in how vehicles are priced. Some used Bentaygas are sold on a VAT-qualifying basis, which carries different implications. We confirm the VAT position on every vehicle we source at the point of enquiry, so you understand exactly what you are paying for.

Container vs RoRo — What We Recommend

For the Bentley Bentayga, our recommendation is unambiguous: dedicated shipping container for all examples. We make this recommendation regardless of the vehicle's age, mileage, or value, for the following reasons:

  • A Bentayga represents a significant investment. The cost premium of a dedicated container over RoRo (typically £800–£1,500 depending on destination) is negligible relative to the vehicle's value.
  • Bentayga bodywork, alloys, and paint finishes are of the highest quality and susceptible to minor but costly damage in RoRo conditions.
  • Handstitched leather interiors and wooden veneers can absorb moisture and salt air in RoRo conditions, particularly on longer routes.
  • Container shipping provides a clean, documented chain of custody that supports insurance claims if anything does go wrong.

V8 vs W12 — Market Preferences by Country

The Bentayga is available with two principal powertrains: the 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 (542 bhp) and the 6.0-litre twin-turbo W12 (626 bhp in Speed guise). Market preferences differ significantly:

  • Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait): Strong preference for W12 and Speed models. Power and prestige hierarchy matters in Gulf markets.
  • Africa (Kenya, Nigeria): V8 is the dominant choice — more practical fuel consumption, more accessible servicing, and represents the majority of available used stock.
  • Southeast Asia (Singapore, Malaysia): V8 is the volume seller; W12 commands a premium but sells well at the top of the market.
  • Caribbean: V8 is most common given fuel costs and availability.

From a resale perspective, W12 models typically command a stronger premium in Middle Eastern markets, while V8 examples have broader liquidity in African and Asian markets.

Popular Export Markets

Bentley Bentaygas are exported from the UK to markets across the globe. Our most active destinations include:

  • Nigeria — Lagos is an active prestige vehicle market. Used Bentaygas with strong specification are in consistent demand.
  • Kenya — Nairobi's prestige market has grown significantly. Subject to 8-year age rule — see our Kenya guide.
  • UAE and Saudi Arabia — Active market, W12 preferred. Buyers often seek bespoke Mulliner specifications.
  • Singapore — Ultra-high-specification examples in immaculate condition, near-new mileage.
  • Malaysia — RHD market, strong Bentley presence, V8 focus.
  • Jamaica and Trinidad — Caribbean luxury market. RHD, drives on left.

Documentation Required

  • V5C logbook (UK registration document)
  • VAT invoice or Margin Scheme invoice from selling dealer
  • Bill of Lading from shipping line
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Marine insurance certificate
  • Bentley Certificate of Conformity (available from Bentley for most markets)
  • Import licence or permit where required by destination country
  • KEBS pre-export inspection certificate (required for Kenya)
