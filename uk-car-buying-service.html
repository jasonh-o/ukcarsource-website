UK Car Finder Service

Tell us the car you want and your budget. We locate suitable cars, arrange an inspection if requested, negotiate the price and connect you with the seller. You buy the car directly from the seller. This service is for cars bought for use in the UK. Buying from overseas or for export? See vehicle sourcing and how it works.

From everyday cars to luxury car sourcing, including Bentley, Porsche, Range Rover and Rolls-Royce, we search the UK market for the right car at the right price.

How It Works

  1. Your brief. Tell us the model, budget and must-haves, or send us a car you've seen.
  2. Search. We find suitable cars and review the advertised details and history.
  3. Inspection. If you ask for one, we arrange an independent inspection and you receive the report.
  4. Negotiation. We negotiate with the seller on your behalf.
  5. Introduction. We connect you with the seller. You agree the sale and pay the seller directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost?

Our fee is 5% of the final vehicle price, with a minimum of £600 per car, including VAT. £150 is paid to start and counts towards the fee. The balance is due when you agree to buy. Inspections, history checks and delivery are charged separately and agreed first.

Who do I buy the car from?

From the seller. We connect you with them, and you agree the sale and pay them directly. UK Car Source is not the seller.

Who is responsible if something goes wrong with the car?

The sale contract is between you and the seller, so any warranty, return or complaint about the car is a matter for the seller. We are responsible for carrying out our service with reasonable care.

Who inspects the car?

An independent inspector. The report comes to you, so you can decide whether to go ahead.

Do you check the vehicle's history?

We review the service and MOT history and ask the seller for their history check (HPI or similar). If there isn't one, we can arrange a finance, write-off and stolen check at cost, agreed with you first.

Can the car be delivered?

Yes. You can arrange your own transport, or we can suggest a transporter.

I'm a dealer. Can I use this service?

Dealer and trade stock orders are quoted individually, depending on volume and the vehicles you need.

Can I cancel?

Full terms, including cancellation, are confirmed in writing before we start.
