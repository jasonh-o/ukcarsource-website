Cost Guide: Tanzania

UK to Tanzania Car Import Cost in 2026

Import duty, excise duty and VAT on a car shipped from the UK to Dar es Salaam stack on top of each other, so for a large new 4x4 they can add around two thirds of the CIF value. This guide sets out each charge in 2026, with a worked example for a new Toyota Land Cruiser.

The Charges at a Glance

ChargeHow it is worked out (November 2026)
Import duty25% of the CIF value (EAC common external tariff)
Excise duty by engine size5% up to 2,000cc and 10% above 2,000cc, charged on the CIF value plus import duty. Cars up to 1,000cc moved from 0% to 5% on 1 July 2026
Extra excise on used carsFrom 1 July 2026: 18% at 8 to 10 years old, 35% over 10 and up to 20 years, 40% over 20 years. Cars under 8 years old do not pay it
VAT18% on the mainland, charged on the CIF value plus duty and excise. 15% in Zanzibar
Railway Development Levy2% of the CIF value
Customs processing fee0.6% of the FOB value
First registrationRegistration tax TZS 250,000, a motor vehicle licence fee of TZS 200,000 to 300,000 and an HIV and AIDS Response Levy of TZS 50,000 to 200,000, both set by engine size
Inspection (used cars)A Certificate of Roadworthiness from EAA Company before shipping, valid for 3 months

Worked Example: New Toyota Land Cruiser

This example is a brand new Toyota Land Cruiser with the 2.8 litre diesel engine (2,755cc), shipped from the UK to Dar es Salaam. We have used a CIF value of £60,000 and an FOB value of £56,500 to keep the sums simple. They are round figures, not a price quote, and TRA works in Tanzanian shillings at its own exchange rate.

ItemAmount
CIF value (car, freight and insurance)£60,000
Import duty (25% of CIF)£15,000
Excise duty (10% of CIF plus duty, engine over 2,000cc)£7,500
VAT (18% of CIF plus duty and excise)£14,850
Railway Development Levy (2% of CIF)£1,200
Customs processing fee (0.6% of FOB)£339
Total taxes£38,889
First registration (in shillings)TZS 750,000: registration tax 250,000, licence fee 300,000 and HIV and AIDS Response Levy 200,000

On these figures the taxes come to about 65% of the CIF value. Because each tax is charged on a base that includes the taxes before it, the total is higher than the headline rates suggest. A used vehicle 8 years old or more would also pay the extra age excise, which is one reason fleets often choose new or nearly new vehicles.

Pickups and minibuses, such as the Toyota Hilux and Commuter, are classed differently from passenger cars and can carry different rates, so we confirm those with your clearing agent before you buy.

Important

This is an illustration, not a quotation. TRA sets the customs value and the exchange rate, and rates can change with each year's Finance Act. Port charges, clearing agent fees, inspection fees, insurance and inland transport are not included. Check the figures with TRA and your clearing agent before you commit.

Where UK Sourcing Helps

Every tax above is a percentage of the CIF value, or of a total built on it, so a lower purchase price saves more than the price difference alone. Choosing a vehicle under 8 years old avoids the extra age excise altogether.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is import duty worked out on a car in Tanzania?

Import duty is 25% of the CIF value (the car plus freight and insurance to Dar es Salaam). Excise duty of 5% or 10%, depending on engine size, is then charged on the CIF value plus duty, and 18% VAT on the CIF value plus duty and excise. A 2% Railway Development Levy and a 0.6% customs processing fee also apply.

Do new cars pay the extra excise for older vehicles?

No. The extra excise only applies to used cars 8 years old or more: from 1 July 2026 it is 18% at 8 to 10 years, 35% over 10 and up to 20 years, and 40% over 20 years.

Do used cars from the UK need an inspection for Tanzania?

Yes. Used vehicles from the UK need a Certificate of Roadworthiness from EAA Company, the inspector appointed by the Tanzania Bureau of Standards, before they are shipped. The certificate is valid for 3 months.

Who pays the duty and taxes?

The buyer, through their own clearing agent, when the car arrives in Tanzania. We quote CFR Dar es Salaam, or CIF including marine insurance on request.
