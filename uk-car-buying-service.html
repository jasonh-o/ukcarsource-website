UK Car Buying Service We Find, Check & Buy It for You

Tell us the car you want and your budget. We search for the best example, check its history, arrange an independent inspection, negotiate the price and organise delivery anywhere in the UK. Clear fees, agreed before we start. This service is for cars bought for use in the UK.

How It Works

  1. Your brief. Tell us the make, model, budget and anything you must have, for example automatic only, ULEZ compliant or no insurance write-off history. If you have already seen a car advertised, send us the link.
  2. The search. We look for the best examples within your budget. If you are flexible on year, engine or colour, we concentrate on condition and history rather than a narrow specification.
  3. History checks. We review the service and MOT history, ownership and condition, and speak to the seller.
  4. Independent inspection. Before you commit, we recommend an independent inspection. If it finds significant problems, we move on to another car rather than risk an expensive mistake.
  5. Purchase and delivery. We negotiate the price, coordinate the purchase and arrange delivery to you.

We would rather recommend a well-maintained car with a strong service history than a newer-looking one with patchy maintenance. That matters most on prestige and performance cars, where repairs are expensive.

History Checks

We ask the selling dealer for their existing history check (HPI or similar), which most dealers can provide free. If one isn't available, or the car is a private sale, we can run a full finance, write-off and stolen-vehicle check for you at cost, confirmed before we order it.

Independent Inspection

An independent inspection typically covers:

  • General mechanical condition and bodywork
  • Visible accident damage and wear
  • A diagnostic scan for recorded fault codes
  • Brake condition and brake fluid
  • Battery condition
  • A road test
  • A detailed written report

Inspections are from around £335 per vehicle, confirmed with the inspection provider before booking. Each car inspected has its own inspection fee. For some cars, such as Porsche and other performance models, we may also suggest a check by a marque specialist.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the car buying service cost?

There is a £99 engagement fee to start, which is credited against the purchasing fee. The purchasing fee is 5% of the final vehicle price, with a minimum of £600. All our fees include VAT. Inspections, history checks, viewings and delivery are quoted separately before you agree to them.

When do I pay the purchasing fee?

Only once, when we have found a suitable vehicle and you are ready to buy it. If a car does not meet expectations, we keep looking without charging another purchasing fee.

Do you check the vehicle's history?

Yes. We review the service and MOT history, ownership and condition. We ask the selling dealer for their existing history check (HPI or similar), which most dealers can provide free. If one is not available, or the car is a private sale, we can run a full finance, write-off and stolen-vehicle check at cost, confirmed before we order it.

Do you recommend an independent inspection?

Yes, before you commit to buy. An independent inspection covers mechanical condition, bodywork, visible accident damage, a diagnostic scan, brakes, the battery and a road test, with a written report. Inspections are from around £335 per vehicle, confirmed with the inspection provider before booking. For some cars we may also suggest a marque specialist check.

Can you deliver the car to me?

Yes, anywhere in the UK. The cost depends on where the car is, and we quote it before arranging transport.

Can you view the car in person?

Yes, if you would like us to. Attendance and travel costs depend on the car's location and are quoted and agreed with you in advance.

I want to use the car for hire. Is there anything to check?

Check your insurance before you buy. Standard private motor insurance generally does not cover self-drive hire, and hire insurers may have rules on a vehicle's age, value or specification.

I'm a dealer. Do these fees apply to me?

No. Dealer and trade stock orders are quoted individually, depending on volume and the vehicles you need.

Can I cancel?

Full terms, including cancellation, are confirmed in writing before you pay the engagement fee.
