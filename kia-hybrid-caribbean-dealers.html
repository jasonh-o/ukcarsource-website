Why Brand New Suits the Islands

Jamaica, Trinidad & Tobago, Barbados, the Bahamas, Guyana and the Eastern Caribbean all drive on the left, so right-hand drive is a direct fit. Most islands also limit the age of imported used cars, and a brand-new car avoids that entirely. See the age rules by island.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order?

The minimum is 2 units, shipped together in a 40ft container. You can mix models and colours, and larger orders ship in additional containers.

Where are the cars built?

They are brand-new right-hand drive vehicles, assembled in Asia and shipped direct to your port. They do not come through the UK.

Do the cars come with a warranty?

No. They are export-market vehicles and are supplied without a manufacturer warranty.

How much is import duty?

Duty and taxes are set by your country and paid locally by the buyer. Check with your customs authority or clearing agent. We quote CFR to your port.
