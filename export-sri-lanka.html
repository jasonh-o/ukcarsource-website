Overview

Exporting to Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka reopened vehicle imports in February 2025, after almost five years of restrictions. With pent-up demand and strict age rules, buyers need recent, well-documented right-hand drive vehicles: exactly what the UK market supplies. Import duties are layered and significant, so accurate CIF valuation and correct documents matter.

We supply private buyers, dealers and fleet operators in Sri Lanka with recent UK cars, SUVs and commercial vehicles, and we accept payment by Letter of Credit (from a bank approved by our UK bank) or by deposit with the balance before shipping.

UK Car Source manages the full export process to Sri Lanka including documentation preparation and sea freight to Colombo, with the document pack your clearing agent needs.

Import Regulations

Import Rules

Drive ConfigurationRight-hand drive required.
Vehicle Age LimitCars and SUVs: no more than 3 years old, whoever imports them. Age is the period from the date of manufacture (shown on the manufacturer's certificate or export inspection certificate) to the Bill of Lading date, not the UK registration date. Pickups, vans and other goods vehicles up to 5 tonnes: no more than 4 years. Minibuses with 13 to 24 seats: no more than 5 years. Heavier trucks and larger buses have their own limits. Importers not registered with the Department of Motor Traffic as vehicle importers can import one vehicle in 12 months, and every vehicle must be registered within 90 days of the customs declaration, or a late fee of 3% of the CIF value a month applies (capped at 45%). Source: Gazette Extraordinary No. 2421/44 of 31 January 2025. We confirm the current rule for your vehicle before purchase.
ConditionRoadworthy condition. Customs inspection on arrival.
Customs AuthoritySri Lanka Customs
Duty & VATCustoms duty, surcharge, excise duty, VAT, SSCL and Luxury Tax apply. Luxury Tax is charged above a set value, so check it before ordering a prestige car.
StandardsUK-specification vehicles generally accepted. Emission standards apply.

Import regulations are subject to change. We recommend confirming current requirements with the relevant customs authority prior to shipment.

Tax & Duties

Import Tax Breakdown

Customs Duty30% general rate, plus a temporary 50% surcharge on that duty, reported to run to 31 December 2026. Confirm the rate before shipping.
VAT18%
SSCL2.5%
Excise DutyA fixed rupee amount per cc, rising with engine size. For example, Rs 12,050 per cc for 3,001 to 4,000 cc petrol cars.
Luxury TaxLuxury Tax is charged above a set value, so check it before ordering a prestige car.
Hybrid / Electric VehiclesExcise for electric cars is charged per kW rather than per cc. Compare the figures with your clearing agent before ordering.
Age RestrictionCars and SUVs no more than 3 years old, from the date of manufacture to the Bill of Lading date. Goods vehicles up to 5 tonnes no more than 4 years. We confirm the rule for your vehicle before purchase.
Customs AuthoritySri Lanka Customs via ASYCUDA++ system

Tax rates are indicative and subject to change. Verify with local customs authority before shipping.

Port Guide

Port Guide

Colombo Port (Sri Lanka Ports Authority) is the main vehicle import terminal. Colombo is one of Asia's busiest transhipment hubs: efficient and well-connected. Jaya Container Terminal (JCT) and South Asia Gateway Terminals (SAGT) handle most containers. Clearance 7–14 working days. Sri Lanka Customs processes via ASYCUDA++ system. Pre-arrival documentation can be submitted. Galle Port is secondary. UK to Colombo sea transit usually takes 3 to 4 weeks.

Market Advice

Local Market Advice

Sri Lanka drives on the left: RHD from UK is ideal. Imports were restricted from 2020 and reopened in February 2025, with a strict age rule: cars and SUVs no more than 3 years old from the date of manufacture. Excise duty is charged per cc for petrol and diesel engines and per kW for electric cars, so engine size makes a big difference to the cost. Ask your clearing agent to compare the figures before you choose a model. Our dealer customers mostly order prestige SUVs such as Range Rover, Defender and the Mercedes-AMG G63. Colombo is the primary market. UK vehicles need to be recent and in excellent condition. Service history from UK dealers adds value.

Buying for family in Sri Lanka? Many of our Sri Lanka enquiries come from Sri Lankans living in the UK who want a car for family at home. We can meet you in the UK, source and inspect the vehicle, and ship it to Colombo in your family's name, with the documents they need for clearance.

Common Questions

Sri Lanka Import FAQ

Can I import a car into Sri Lanka now?

Yes. Sri Lanka reopened vehicle imports in February 2025 after the restrictions introduced in 2020. Cars and SUVs must be no more than 3 years old, counted from the date of manufacture to the Bill of Lading date. Pickups and other goods vehicles have their own limits. We check the current rule for your exact vehicle before buying.

Can I pay with a Letter of Credit?

Yes. We accept Letters of Credit issued by banks approved by our UK bank. Tell us your bank before opening the LC. We also offer a deposit with the balance paid before shipping. How LC payment works.

How much tax do I pay to import a car into Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka applies customs duty, surcharge, excise duty, VAT, SSCL and Luxury Tax: total effective duties can be substantial, and the structure heavily favours hybrid and electric vehicles. Luxury Tax is charged above a set value, so check it before ordering a prestige car.

Do UK cars need to be converted for Sri Lanka?

No. Sri Lanka drives on the left and UK right-hand drive vehicles are ideal as standard. No steering conversion is needed or offered.

Which prestige vehicles are most viable for Sri Lanka?

Our dealer customers mostly order prestige SUVs such as Range Rover, Range Rover Sport, Defender and the Mercedes-AMG G63. Excise duty rises with engine size, so ask your clearing agent to compare models before you order.
