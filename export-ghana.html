Overview

Exporting to Ghana

Ghana has a well-established vehicle import market with Tema Port being the main entry point. Imports are regulated by the Ghana Revenue Authority. Vehicles must comply with roadworthiness standards and age restrictions apply to certain vehicle categories.

UK Car Source handles the entire export process including vehicle sourcing, documentation, sea freight and customs support. RoRo shipping is the most common and cost-effective method to Ghana.

Port Guide

Port Guide

Tema Port (near Accra) is Ghana's main deep-water port and the primary vehicle import hub, operated by Ghana Ports and Harbours Authority (GPHA). The port has modern facilities including RoRo and container terminals with a dedicated Vehicle Inspection and Clearing Terminal (VICT).

Ghana Customs processes clearance through the ICUMS system — electronic pre-arrival submission is required. Typical clearance takes 7–14 working days. Takoradi Port (secondary) handles some imports for western Ghana. Budget for destination inspection by an approved agency (GIS). A freight forwarding agent with Ghana experience is strongly recommended.

Market Advice

Local Market Advice

Accra is the primary market. The Ghanaian prestige vehicle market is growing rapidly, driven by the oil and gas sector in Takoradi and remittances from the diaspora. UK-sourced vehicles are respected for quality. 4WDs dominate outside Accra — Accra city traffic is severe and fuel efficiency matters.

Diaspora buyers often source via UK agents when visiting or returning home. Parts availability is best for Toyota; European brands are available through authorised dealers in Accra. White and silver are the preferred colours. The market has a strong preference for automatic transmission — manual gearbox vehicles are harder to sell.
