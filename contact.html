Common Questions

Before You Enquire

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?

We aim to respond to all enquiries within a few hours during business hours. For urgent requirements, WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us directly.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

The more detail the better — vehicle make, model, year, specification, colour preference, budget and your destination if you're looking to export. This allows us to give you a fast, accurate response.

Do you work with overseas buyers?

Yes. The majority of our enquiries come from international clients looking to import a car from the UK. We work with buyers in Jamaica, Kenya, Singapore, Thailand, South Africa and across the Caribbean, Africa and SE Asia.

Is there an obligation when I make an enquiry?

No. Your initial enquiry is completely free and without obligation. We'll confirm whether we can source your vehicle, provide a cost estimate and timeline, and you decide whether to proceed.

Can you source and export in the same service?

Yes. UK Car Source provides a combined sourcing and export service — we find the right vehicle in the UK, handle all export documentation and arrange international shipping to your destination, all under one roof.

Do you work with trade buyers and dealerships?

Yes. We supply private individuals, trade buyers, dealerships and fleet clients. For regular trade enquiries or multi-vehicle requirements, contact us directly to discuss an ongoing supply arrangement.
