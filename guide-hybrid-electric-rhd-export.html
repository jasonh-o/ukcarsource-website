Export Guide

Hybrid and Electric
Right-Hand Drive from the UK

The UK has a wide choice of new and used right-hand drive hybrid and electric cars. This guide covers what to check before you export one.

Which Type

Hybrid, Plug-in or Electric

The right choice depends on how you drive, the charging where you live and how your country taxes each type.

Hybrid

A petrol engine with a small battery that charges itself. No plug needed, so it suits places with little public charging.

Plug-in hybrid

A larger battery you charge from a plug, for shorter trips on electric power, with a petrol engine for longer journeys.

Electric

Battery only, charged from a plug. Lowest running costs where charging is easy, and often the lowest import tax.

Duty and Tax

How Countries Tax Them

Many countries tax hybrid and electric cars differently to petrol and diesel cars. Some examples from our country pages:

Kenya

Excise duty on electric cars is 0%, against 20% to 35% for petrol and diesel. Kenya guide →

Maldives

Electric cars pay 10% duty, against 100% on other passenger cars. Maldives guide →

Malta

Registration tax is based on CO2, so low-emission hybrids and electric cars generally pay less. Malta guide →

Singapore

Our worked example shows an electric car costing S$42,500 less in taxes and COE. Singapore cost guide →

Sri Lanka

Hybrid and electric models can attract lower duty. Sri Lanka guide →

Rules change often. Check current rates with your clearing agent before you order. Duty and local taxes are paid by the buyer on arrival.

Before You Buy

What to Check

Battery health. On a used car, ask for a battery health report. We can arrange an inspection through our specialist partners.

Charging. UK cars use the Type 2 connector for AC charging and CCS2 for DC rapid charging. Check which connectors are used where you live.

Import rules. Check your country's age limit and whether it treats a UK-registered car as used. Our country pages cover the main rules.

Warranty and servicing. UK-specification cars are supplied for export without manufacturer warranty. Servicing and parts are arranged locally by the buyer. We can also supply parts from the UK.

Apps and connected services. Some are set up for the UK and may not work in your country.

Shipping. Shipping lines have rules for cars with lithium batteries, such as a limit on the charge level when the car is loaded. We coordinate inspections and shipping through specialist partners, and your own clearing agent handles customs.

Questions

Hybrid and Electric FAQ

Are hybrid and electric cars cheaper to import?

Often, but not always. Many countries charge lower duty or tax on hybrid and electric cars than on petrol and diesel cars, but the rules differ by country and change often. Check current rates with your clearing agent before you order.

What charging connector do UK electric cars use?

UK cars use the Type 2 connector for AC charging and CCS2 for DC rapid charging. Check which connectors are used where you live before you order.

Can I check the battery on a used car?

Yes. Ask for a battery health report before you buy. We can arrange an inspection through our specialist partners.

Do new UK cars come with a warranty?

UK-specification cars supplied for export on our export price list come without manufacturer warranty. Servicing and parts are arranged locally by the buyer, and we can supply parts from the UK.

Can hybrid and electric cars be shipped?

Yes. Shipping lines have rules for cars with lithium batteries, such as a limit on the charge level when the car is loaded. We coordinate inspections and shipping through specialist partners, and your own clearing agent handles customs.
