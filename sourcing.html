What Is Vehicle Sourcing?

Vehicle sourcing means finding and securing a specific vehicle on your behalf — one that matches your exact requirements for make, model, specification, colour and budget. Rather than visiting multiple dealerships or browsing listings, you work with a specialist who accesses the full market on your behalf, including dealer networks, factory order channels, specialist suppliers and auction houses.

UK Car Source provides professional vehicle sourcing for private individuals, trade buyers and international clients. We have established relationships with main dealer groups, franchise partners and specialist suppliers across the UK and internationally — giving our clients access to a far wider pool of vehicles than any single dealership or forecourt.

Where We Source From

Frequently Asked Questions — Vehicle Sourcing

What vehicles can you source?
UK Car Source sources prestige saloons, performance cars, luxury SUVs, specialist imports, factory-order new vehicles and collector cars. We work across all premium brands including Porsche, Bentley, Rolls-Royce, Mercedes-AMG, BMW M, Range Rover, Lamborghini, Ferrari and many more.
Can you source left-hand drive vehicles?
Yes. UK Car Source sources both RHD and LHD vehicles. Left-hand drive models are sourced from Europe, the USA and the UAE for clients in continental Europe, the Middle East and other LHD markets.
Can you source vehicles from Japan?
Yes. We source vehicles from Japan through established dealer and auction partnerships. Popular Japanese-market vehicles include the Nissan GT-R, Toyota Land Cruiser, Lexus models and limited-edition performance cars unavailable in Western markets.
What is your sourcing fee?
UK Car Source charges a transparent sourcing fee agreed before any work begins. The total cost — vehicle price plus our fee — is presented to you in full before you commit. There are no hidden charges.
How do I start the process?
Simply contact us with your requirements — the more detail the better. We'll confirm whether we can source your vehicle, provide an estimated timeline and cost, and begin the search once you're happy to proceed.
