Export Guide

Exporting a Range Rover Autobiography from the UK

The Range Rover Autobiography is consistently one of the most exported prestige vehicles from the UK — and for good reason. Built in Solihull, England, with UK factory pricing that sits well below what the same vehicle costs through an authorised dealer in most export markets, it represents one of the best-value export propositions in the luxury SUV segment.

Why the Range Rover Autobiography is One of the UK's Most Exported Prestige SUVs

The Range Rover has a global reputation that transcends markets. It is aspirational in Lagos, essential in Nairobi, fashionable in Singapore, and prestigious in the Gulf. More importantly, it is manufactured in the UK — and competitive factory pricing, combined with the depth of the UK used market and favourable exchange rates, makes it significantly cheaper to source here than almost anywhere else in the world.

The Autobiography trim represents the top of the standard Range Rover lineup (below the SV), and it is the sweet spot for export buyers: high enough specification to be genuinely impressive, widely available in both new and used markets, and supported by an excellent global dealer network for after-sales service. Buyers know what they are getting, and they know its value internationally.

Used Range Rover Autobiographies in the UK are particularly well-represented. The UK domestic market churns through these vehicles at a pace — finance deals, fleet cycling, and a culture of changing vehicles regularly means there is a consistent supply of 1–3 year old examples with full Land Rover service histories, low mileage, and a wide range of colours and specifications. This depth of supply depresses UK used prices in a way that is simply not replicated in most export destination markets.

UK Pricing vs Overseas

A new Range Rover Autobiography in the UK (2026 model, SWB) lists from approximately £150,000 including VAT. Long wheelbase versions start from approximately £163,000. Fully optioned examples — with 4-seat interior, privacy glass suite, panoramic roof, and Meridian Signature audio — can approach £195,000 including VAT. Prices are indicative and should be confirmed at the point of enquiry.

Compare this to what the same vehicle costs in RHD destination markets:

  • Kenya: a similar specification Range Rover Autobiography sourced locally typically costs the equivalent of £180,000–£240,000 once import duties and local taxes are accounted for. Buying direct from the UK is often meaningfully cheaper even after clearing duties.
  • Nigeria: the luxury car market in Lagos runs primarily on UK imports — the UK price advantage is significant, particularly on used vehicles.
  • Singapore: the COE system inflates all vehicle prices substantially. A Range Rover Autobiography in Singapore commonly sells for the equivalent of £250,000–£350,000. Buyers who source from the UK save materially even after duties and COE.
  • Malaysia: import duties apply but UK pricing remains competitive, and right-hand drive availability from the UK is ideal for this market.

On the used side, a 2022 Range Rover Autobiography LWB with 20,000 miles in the UK might sell for £110,000–£130,000. That same vehicle in Nigeria or Kenya, post-import, commands a premium well above its UK acquisition cost.

Right-Hand Drive — The UK Advantage

The UK is one of the world's most important sources of right-hand drive prestige vehicles. All Range Rovers produced for the UK domestic market are RHD, which makes them directly compatible with the majority of our export destinations. These include:

  • East Africa: Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe
  • West Africa: Nigeria, Ghana
  • Southeast Asia: Singapore, Malaysia
  • The Caribbean: Jamaica, Barbados, Trinidad & Tobago, Bahamas, St Lucia, St Kitts
  • Indian Ocean: Mauritius, Seychelles, Maldives
  • South Asia: Sri Lanka, Bangladesh
  • Mediterranean: Cyprus, Malta

Nigeria and Ghana officially drive on the right, but RHD vehicles are widely accepted and actively sought in both markets due to established import patterns from the UK. Many buyers there specifically request UK RHD stock.

Shipping Options

Unlike the Rolls-Royce Cullinan, the Range Rover Autobiography is a vehicle for which RoRo shipping is an acceptable option in certain circumstances:

MethodBest ForTypical Cost
RoRo (Roll-on/Roll-off)Used vehicles, 3+ years old, where cost-efficiency is a priority£800–£1,800 depending on destination
Shared container (groupage)Used vehicles where slight additional protection is desired£1,200–£2,400 depending on destination
Dedicated 20ft containerNew or near-new vehicles, high-spec examples, any buyer wanting maximum protection£2,000–£3,800 depending on destination

Our strong recommendation is container shipping for any Range Rover Autobiography that is less than 3 years old or valued above £100,000 in the UK. The cost difference between RoRo and a dedicated container is modest relative to the vehicle's value, and container shipping eliminates the small but real risk of transit damage.

Popular Destination Markets

We export Range Rover Autobiographies regularly to the following markets:

  • Kenya — one of our highest-volume destinations. KEBS pre-export inspection required. Strong used Range Rover market with consistent demand across all specification levels.
  • Nigeria — Lagos is a major market for used prestige vehicles. Mileage, condition, and colour are key purchase criteria.
  • Singapore — buyers typically seek very low mileage, recent year models, and exceptional specification. The COE system makes UK sourcing particularly attractive.
  • Malaysia — strong appetite for Range Rover across all trim levels. Import duties apply but UK sourcing remains competitive.
  • Caribbean — Jamaica, Barbados, Trinidad & Tobago, Bahamas. Left-hand traffic, RHD compatible, strong demand for Autobiography specifically.
  • Bangladesh — regulated import market where documentation must be thorough. RHD and left-hand traffic.
  • East Africa more broadly — Tanzania, Uganda, and Zambia follow similar import patterns to Kenya. RHD, drives on left.

Age and Specification Considerations

Many destination countries impose age restrictions on imported vehicles. Kenya's 8-year rule is perhaps the most well-known — see our dedicated Kenya import guide for the full detail. For most markets, sourcing a vehicle that is 3 years old or less avoids any age-restriction complications and ensures the vehicle is recent enough to command good resale value in the destination market.

Specification preferences vary by market. In Kenya and Nigeria, white, black, and silver are the most sought-after exterior colours. In Singapore and Malaysia, buyers tend to prioritise very recent model years and low mileage above all else. The 4-seat interior configuration commands a premium across Southeast Asia and the Caribbean. Air suspension, panoramic roofs, and head-up displays are universally valued across all markets. A full Land Rover service history significantly strengthens resale value in every destination we serve.
