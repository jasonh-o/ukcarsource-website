Frequently Asked Questions

Are the cars new?

They are in brand-new condition with delivery mileage only. Each car is registered in the UK before export as part of the supply terms.

What does the export price include?

The car, delivery charged by the manufacturer, first registration and first-year road tax. UK VAT, inland logistics and shipping are excluded, and we quote these to your port.

Can I choose the colour and specification?

It depends on the stock available when you enquire. Tell us your preference and we will send you matching cars.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can order one car or several, across different brands.

Can you supply parts?

Yes, we can supply parts from the UK. The cars are supplied without manufacturer warranty.
