The Process

5 Steps to Your Vehicle

  1. 01
    Enquiry

    Tell Us What You Want

    Contact our team with your vehicle requirements — make, model, specification, colour, optional equipment and budget. The more detail you provide, the faster and more accurately we can source the right vehicle for you. There's no obligation at this stage.

  2. 02
    Sourcing

    We Source Your Vehicle

    Our team contacts our established network of main dealers, franchise groups, specialist suppliers and auction channels across the UK and internationally. We locate vehicles that match your specification and present you with the best options available — including pricing, condition and availability details.

  3. 03
    Secure

    Secure With a Deposit

    Once you've approved the vehicle, a deposit secures it while we complete documentation, history checks and logistics arrangements. We keep you updated throughout and confirm all costs in full before you commit to the balance.

  4. 04
    Payment

    Arrange Payment

    We guide you through the payment process securely and transparently. For international clients, we can work with your bank or payment provider as required. There are no hidden fees — the price agreed is the price you pay.

  5. 05
    Delivery

    Take Delivery of Your Vehicle

    For UK deliveries, we arrange transport to your door. For international clients, we manage the complete export process — export documentation (V5, certificates of conformity), freight booking, shipping and customs clearance guidance at your destination. We don't consider the job done until your vehicle is safely delivered.

Common Questions

Process FAQ

Is there a fee to use UK Car Source?
UK Car Source charges a sourcing fee which is agreed transparently before any work begins. There are no hidden charges. Our fee is included in the total cost presented to you before you commit.
How long does it take to source a vehicle?
Many vehicles can be located within days. Specialist, limited-production or factory-order vehicles may take 2–12 weeks. We provide realistic timelines at the point of enquiry.
Can you source vehicles for export?
Yes. UK Car Source handles the complete sourcing and export process for international clients — UK purchase, export documentation, freight booking and shipping to over 35 countries worldwide.
Do you carry out history and condition checks?
Yes. All vehicles sourced through UK Car Source are subject to a history check as standard. For pre-owned vehicles, we can arrange independent inspections prior to purchase.
Can you source factory-order vehicles?
Yes. Through our dealer network we can access factory-order allocation for Porsche, Bentley, Mercedes-AMG, Land Rover, BMW M and more.
Do you work with trade buyers?
Yes. We work with private individuals, dealerships, fleet buyers and international trade partners — from single vehicle enquiries to multi-vehicle consignments.
