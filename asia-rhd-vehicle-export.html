Asia — Right-Hand Drive Markets

UK Right-Hand Drive Vehicles for Asia

Much of South and Southeast Asia drives on the left. That makes the UK — Europe's largest right-hand drive market — a natural source of well-documented prestige cars, 4x4s and commercial vehicles. UK Car Export, the export division of UK Car Source, supplies private buyers, dealers, parallel importers and fleet operators across the region.

Why Source From the UK

  • Right-hand drive as standard — no conversion, correct for local registration and resale.
  • European prestige depth — the widest RHD supply of Range Rover, Porsche, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Bentley and Rolls-Royce outside their home markets.
  • Documented histories — UK dealer service records and annual roadworthiness tests.
  • Commercial and fleet supply — trucks, vans, pickups, buses and plant. See commercial vehicle export.

For Dealers and Parallel Importers

We work with recond dealers in Malaysia, parallel importers in Thailand and Singapore, licensed importers in Indonesia and dealers in Sri Lanka and Bangladesh. Send us your wanted list and we will source to specification, with history and condition reports before purchase.

Paying Safely

We accept payment by Letter of Credit issued by a bank approved by our UK bank, or by deposit with the balance paid before shipping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Asian countries use right-hand drive vehicles?

Right-hand drive markets we serve include Sri Lanka, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Brunei, Timor-Leste, Hong Kong, Macao, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nepal, the Maldives and Papua New Guinea.

Do you supply dealers and parallel importers in Asia?

Yes. We source to dealer wanted lists for recond dealers, parallel importers and licensed importers, with history and condition reports before purchase.

Can I pay by Letter of Credit?

Yes, from a bank approved by our UK bank. We also accept a deposit with the balance paid before shipping.
