**POST 7 — Educational (Shipping Options)**

---

RoRo vs Container — what's right for your next import?

**RoRo (Roll-on, Roll-off)**
→ Lower cost
→ Faster loading / unloading
→ Vehicle is exposed on deck
→ Better for high-volume standard vehicles

**Container (20ft or 40ft)**
→ Higher cost
→ Maximum physical protection
→ Better for prestige, classic and high-value vehicles
→ Can consolidate multiple units in one container

**Air Freight**
→ Premium cost
→ Fastest option globally
→ For ultra-high-value or time-critical shipments only

Most of our prestige exports travel by container. For a £180,000 Rolls-Royce, the extra cost is noise.

Any questions on shipping for your market? Drop them below.

Exporting to Singapore requires knowing one thing above everything else:

The Certificate of Entitlement.

Singapore's COE system means buyers pay a premium — often £30,000–£60,000+ — simply for the right to own a vehicle. Which changes the buying calculus entirely.

When you've already committed that sum to a certificate, the vehicle itself needs to be exactly right. Wrong spec or mediocre condition isn't an option at that price point.

Singapore dealers we work with are among the most specification-precise buyers we deal with. And rightly so.

If you're exporting to Singapore, understand COE before you understand anything else.
