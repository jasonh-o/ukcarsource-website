Common Questions

Sourcing FAQ

What vehicles can you source?
UK Car Source sources prestige saloons, performance cars, luxury SUVs, specialist imports, factory-order new vehicles and collector cars. We work with all premium brands including Porsche, Bentley, Rolls-Royce, Mercedes-AMG, BMW M, Range Rover and more.
Can you source left-hand drive vehicles?
Yes. UK Car Source sources both RHD and LHD vehicles. Left-hand drive models are sourced from Europe, the USA and the UAE for clients in continental Europe, the Middle East and other LHD markets.
Can you source vehicles from Japan?
Yes. We source vehicles from Japan through established dealer and auction partnerships. Popular Japanese-market vehicles include the Nissan GT-R, Toyota Land Cruiser, Lexus models and limited-edition performance cars unavailable in Western markets.
How do I start the process?
Simply contact us with your requirements — the more detail the better. We'll confirm whether we can source your vehicle, provide an estimated timeline and cost, and begin the search once you're happy to proceed.
