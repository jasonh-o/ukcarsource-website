Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vehicle sourcing agent?
A vehicle sourcing agent finds and secures a specific vehicle on your behalf, accessing dealer networks, auctions and international markets that private buyers cannot easily reach. UK Car Source manages the entire process from finding the right vehicle to arranging delivery.
Can you export a car from the UK to another country?
Yes. UK Car Source exports vehicles to over 35 countries worldwide including Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Kenya, Nigeria, Jamaica, Barbados and many more. We manage documentation, customs and shipping end-to-end.
Do you supply right-hand drive and left-hand drive vehicles?
Yes. UK Car Source specialises in both RHD and LHD vehicles sourced from the UK, Europe, Japan, Australia, UAE and North America.
How long does it take to source a vehicle?
Sourcing typically takes 1–3 weeks depending on specification and availability. For factory orders, lead times vary by manufacturer. Export delivery adds sea freight transit time of typically 2–8 weeks depending on destination.
Do you work with trade buyers as well as private clients?
Yes. UK Car Source works with private individuals, dealerships, fleet buyers and international trade partners. We handle single vehicle enquiries and multi-vehicle consignments.
