We Source to Your Specification

UK Car Source does not carry forecourt stock in the traditional sense. Instead, we locate and secure vehicles specifically to your requirements — colour, specification, optional equipment, mileage and condition. This approach means every vehicle we supply is matched to what the client actually wants, rather than what happens to be available.

Our sourcing network spans main dealer groups, franchise partners, specialist suppliers and auction channels across the UK, Europe, Japan, Australia, UAE and North America. Whether you're looking for a specific new model allocation, a nearly-new example with low delivery mileage, or an older specialist vehicle, we have the connections to find it.

Enquiries are welcome from private individuals, trade buyers and international clients. See how the process works or get in touch directly below.
