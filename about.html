Our Story

Who We Are

UK Car Source is an vehicle sourcing and export specialist with over 15 years of experience supplying carefully selected vehicles to clients across the UK and international markets.

Working with an established network of main dealers, franchise groups and specialist suppliers, we help clients secure high-quality vehicles with transparency, efficiency and attention to detail throughout the process.

From prestige and performance vehicles to factory-order specifications and specialist imports, we provide tailored sourcing solutions for private buyers, trade professionals and overseas partners.

Our approach is built on long-term relationships, trusted industry connections and a straightforward service designed to make vehicle purchasing and export seamless from enquiry through to delivery.
