Exporting to Antigua and Barbuda

Common Questions

Antigua Import FAQ

What taxes apply to a car imported to Antigua?

Import duty, the Revenue Recovery Charge and ABST. Your clearing agent confirms the current rates for your car. Duty and taxes are paid by the buyer locally.

Do UK cars suit Antigua and Barbuda?

Yes. Antigua and Barbuda drive on the left, so UK right-hand drive cars suit the roads.

Do you supply dealers in Antigua?

Yes. We source to order for dealers and private buyers, with payment by bank transfer or Letter of Credit.

Do you ship to Barbuda?

We quote to St John's, Antigua. Your agent can arrange onward delivery.
