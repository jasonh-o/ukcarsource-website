Frequently Asked Questions

Can you supply new Toyota Hilux pickups?

Yes, we take enquiries for the new Hilux Travo Overland. Tell us the specification, quantity and destination port, and we will confirm availability and quote for your order.

Can you export Toyota Commuter minibuses to Africa?

We quote for Commuter minibuses to African ports including Mombasa and Dar es Salaam. Import rules differ by country, so please check that the vehicle can be registered in your market before ordering.

Can I order a single vehicle or multiple vehicles?

Tell us the quantity you need. Whether a single vehicle or a larger order is possible depends on the supplier and the shipping arrangement, and we confirm this for each enquiry.

Can you arrange shipping to Mombasa, Dar es Salaam, Georgetown or Kingston?

We can quote for shipping to these ports. The shipping method, departure port and timing are confirmed on your quotation.

Do you accept Letters of Credit?

Payment can be discussed as bank transfer (TT) or Letter of Credit. Both are subject to supplier and bank acceptance, and we confirm the terms for each order.

Can you provide CFR or CIF pricing?

Yes. We quote CFR (also called CNF) to your destination port. CIF, including marine insurance, is available on request.

Are the vehicles right-hand drive?

Yes. These are right-hand drive vehicles, and the specification of each vehicle is confirmed on your quotation.

Are these vehicles covered by a manufacturer warranty?

You should assume not. Export vehicles are generally supplied without a Toyota manufacturer warranty in the destination country. Anything that applies to a specific vehicle is confirmed before you order.

Who is responsible for import duty, local compliance and registration?

The buyer. Import duty, taxes, local compliance and registration are handled locally. Check the current rules with your customs authority or clearing agent before ordering.
