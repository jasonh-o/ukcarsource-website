Step by Step

How to Export a Car from the UK

UK Car Source manages the entire UK car export process on your behalf. Here is how we export a car from the UK from start to finish — whether you are a private buyer importing a car from the UK, a trade buyer or an international dealership.

01

Tell Us What You Need

Contact our UK car export team with your vehicle requirements, destination country and budget. We confirm availability, costs and timelines — no obligation.

02

Vehicle Sourced in the UK

We locate and secure the right vehicle from our UK dealer network, auction channels or specialist suppliers. All vehicles are inspected and history-checked before export.

03

UK Export Documentation

We prepare all UK car export paperwork — V5C logbook, DVLA notification, export invoice, bill of lading, certificate of conformity and destination-specific customs documents.

04

Shipping from the UK

Your vehicle is loaded at a UK port via RoRo or container. We work with trusted UK freight partners to ensure secure, insured transit to your destination port worldwide.

05

Customs Clearance

We provide full customs clearance guidance for your destination country. For regular trade clients, we can coordinate directly with your nominated clearing agent to streamline import.

06

Delivery & Support

We track your shipment and keep you updated throughout transit. Our team remains available for post-delivery support with any queries on documentation or registration in your country.

Common Questions

UK Car Export FAQ

How do I export a car from the UK?

Contact UK Car Source with your vehicle requirements and destination. We handle everything — sourcing, export documentation, V5 deregistration, shipping booking and customs clearance guidance. You receive your vehicle at destination without dealing with any of the paperwork.

What documents are needed to export a car from the UK?

Key documents include the V5C logbook, bill of sale, export invoice, packing list and letter of authority. Some destinations require a Certificate of Conformity or additional homologation paperwork. UK Car Source prepares and manages all documentation as part of our service.

How much does it cost to export a car from the UK?

Costs depend on the vehicle, destination and shipping method. RoRo is typically the most affordable option; container shipping offers more protection for high-value vehicles. UK Car Source provides fully transparent quotes covering all shipping, documentation and logistics costs upfront.

What is RoRo shipping for UK car export?

RoRo (Roll-on Roll-off) means your vehicle is driven directly onto a specialist cargo vessel — no container required. It is usually the most cost-effective method for exporting a car from the UK and is available to most major destinations in Africa, the Caribbean and SE Asia.

Can you export right-hand drive cars from the UK?

Yes. The UK is one of the world's largest sources of RHD vehicles. UK Car Source specialises in right-hand drive car export to Jamaica, Kenya, Singapore, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Bangladesh, Australia and all RHD markets worldwide.

How long does UK car export take?

Transit times vary by destination. Caribbean destinations typically take 3–5 weeks by sea. West Africa takes 2–4 weeks. SE Asia (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand) takes 4–6 weeks. Air freight is available for urgent shipments at a premium. UK Car Source confirms exact timelines at the point of enquiry.
