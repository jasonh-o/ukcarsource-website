Why We Track the Market

UK Car Source sources and exports premium vehicles to clients across the UK, Caribbean, Africa, South East Asia and the Middle East. Staying informed on new model launches, specification changes, residual values and market trends is central to sourcing the right vehicle at the right time. The reviews and updates featured here are curated specifically around the brands and models our clients ask about most.

Models We Follow

Our sourcing work covers a broad range of premium and prestige vehicles. We pay close attention to the Porsche 911 and Cayenne ranges, the Range Rover Sport and SV, Mercedes-AMG models including the G63, the Bentley Bentayga and Continental GT, Toyota Land Cruiser variants, and Rolls-Royce across all current models. We also track emerging performance vehicles, limited editions and factory-allocation models that our clients frequently request.

Export Market Intelligence

For clients importing cars from the UK, understanding current pricing, specification availability and new model timelines is essential. UK Car Source advises clients in Jamaica, Kenya, Singapore, Thailand, South Africa and across the Caribbean on the best time to source specific models — particularly where new model cycles affect residual values and specification availability in the UK market.
