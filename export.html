Common Questions

UK Car Export FAQ

Can I buy a UK car for export?

Yes. Tell us the make, model, specification and budget and we source the exact vehicle to order from UK dealers, auctions and trade contacts, with photos of the exact car from the selling dealer before you commit. See why UK prestige cars are often cheaper.

How do I pay for a car exported from the UK?

By Letter of Credit issued by a bank approved by our UK bank, or by deposit to secure the vehicle with the balance paid before shipping.

Do you handle shipping all the way to my port?

Yes. We arrange the purchase, UK export paperwork, transport to the UK port and RoRo or container shipping to your port. Marine insurance is available on CIF terms or on request. Your own clearing agent handles customs.

What documents are needed to export a car from the UK?

The V5C logbook, bill of sale, export invoice, packing list and letter of authority. Some countries also need a Certificate of Conformity, supplied where available. Read how to export a car from the UK.

How much does it cost to export a car from the UK?

It depends on the vehicle, destination and shipping method. RoRo is usually the cheapest, and a container gives more protection. We quote the car and shipping together. Duty and local taxes are paid by you in your country.

How long does UK car export take?

It depends on your port, the sailing and the vehicle. We confirm the expected timing when we quote.
