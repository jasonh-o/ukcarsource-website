Export Process

How We Export Your Land Rover

UK Car Export handles every aspect of the Land Rover and Range Rover export process — from sourcing the exact specification, colour and options you require through to delivery at your destination port or address.

Range Rover and Defender exports are in exceptionally high demand across Jamaica, Kenya, Nigeria, Trinidad, Singapore, Malaysia and South Africa. We source both new factory-order vehicles and approved pre-owned examples to meet client requirements at any budget.

01

Enquiry & Specification

Tell us your exact requirements — model, specification, colour, year and budget. We'll confirm availability and pricing.

02

Vehicle Sourcing

We source your Land Rover from authorised UK Jaguar Land Rover dealers and vetted specialist suppliers, providing a full vehicle report.

03

Export Documentation

We prepare all DVLA export documentation, V5 deregistration, invoice and packing lists for your destination country.

04

Shipping & Logistics

Your vehicle is transported to port and shipped by RORO or container freight — whichever is most suitable for your destination.

05

Arrival & Customs

We provide all documentation required for customs clearance at your destination, including commercial invoice and Bill of Lading.
